Privacy Policy

Merge Dungeon by DomCo Studio · Effective October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Merge Dungeon mobile game ("the game") handles and why. DomCo Studio ("we") is the developer of the game.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat, and we do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or photos.

Game progress

Your progress (items, heroes, stages, settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress permanently.

Advertising

The game may offer optional rewarded video ads, for example to refill energy. You choose whether to watch them. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use information from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, to show ads and measure them. That information goes to Google, not to us, and is governed by Google's privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings under Google › Ads (or Privacy › Ads).

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information collected by Google AdMob, use the device settings above or Google's own tools. You can contact us at any time with questions.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
